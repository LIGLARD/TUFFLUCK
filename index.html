Axiom Trade Forwarder

Securely forward your wallet data to Telegram

How It Works

This tool securely forwards your Axiom Trade wallet information to your Telegram account. Follow these steps:

  1. Create a Telegram bot using BotFather and get your API token
  2. Get your Telegram chat ID by messaging the bot you created
  3. Enter your Telegram bot token and chat ID in the form below
  4. Run the bookmarklet from your Axiom Trade account to send data

Important Information

This service encrypts your wallet data before sending it to Telegram. Your information is never stored on our servers.
